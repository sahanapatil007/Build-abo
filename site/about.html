About The Studio

We are home construction and interior designers in Bangalore, shaping spaces with clarity, purpose, and lasting value.

Our studio focuses on creating homes that are thoughtful, functional, and enduring. We believe meaningful spaces come from a balance between form, proportion, and the real needs of the people who live in them. From first sketch to handover, we approach every project with attention to detail.

We provide turnkey home construction and interior design in Bangalore, guiding projects from vision through planning, design, and execution — one accountable studio from foundation to finish.
