Lucas Bennett

ALSHA’s CEO & Founder

Lucas Bennett is the CEO & Founder of ALSHA, leading the studio with a clear vision for thoughtful, human-centered architecture. With extensive experience in both residential and commercial projects, he guides the creative direction of the practice while ensuring every design balances innovation, functionality, and long-term value.

He works closely with architects, designers, and clients throughout the entire process — from early concepts to final execution — shaping ideas into refined architectural solutions while maintaining clarity, precision, and a strong design identity.
